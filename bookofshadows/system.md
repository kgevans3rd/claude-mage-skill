# The Book of Shadows: The Mage Players Guide

A **bring-your-own-rulebook** module for the Mage 2nd Edition players guide. It
ships the shape of what the book adds and cites its pages; the values come from
your own copy.

Citations are to **printed** page numbers. In the reference scan, PDF page =
printed page + 1.

## Why this module exists

Merits and Flaws are not in the Mage 2nd Edition core rulebook. That book's own
index runs *Methodologies → Mistridge* with no "Merits", and *First Cabal →
Foci* with no "Flaws". They are here, in the players guide, across printed
pages 29–46.

## Merits and Flaws

Merits are bought with freebie points. Flaws hand freebie points back, which is
why they are capped — without a ceiling a character could buy an arbitrary
number of drawbacks and spend the proceeds on Spheres.

The system is optional, and the Storyteller decides whether it is in play at
all before anyone builds a character around it.

{{bos:table:merits_flaws@p.29-46}}

Only name, cost, type and category are carried here. Each entry's effect is a
paragraph of the book's own prose — look it up on the cited page.

Two entries are worth knowing about before you use the table:

- **Spirit Magnet** is the only entry that can be taken as either a Merit
  (3–7 points) or a Flaw (2–6). Its `type` says so.
- **Magical Prohibition or Imperative** (2–7) is priced by the Storyteller
  against the severity of the *geas* and of the consequence for breaking it,
  rather than picked freely off a list.

## Mutually exclusive pairs

Several entries forbid each other. The book states these inline rather than in
a chart, so they are not a table here, but they are worth knowing:

- Concentration (Merit) excludes Absent-Minded (Flaw)
- Reputation (Merit) excludes Notoriety (Flaw)
- Acute Senses (Merit) excludes Hard of Hearing and Bad Sight (Flaws)
- Double-Jointed (Merit) excludes Lame and Paraplegic (Flaws)
- Ambidextrous (Merit) excludes One Arm (Flaw)
- Higher Purpose (Merit) excludes Driving Goal (Flaw)
